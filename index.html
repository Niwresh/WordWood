📖 How To Play

Select letters on the 8×10 board by dragging (mouse or touch). Adjacent letters connect horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Form words of 3+ letters. Longer words score more!

Special tiles:

  • 🟡 Gold — bonus points
  • 🌈 Rainbow — wildcard letter
  • 💣 Bomb — clears nearby tiles
  • ⭐ Star — score multiplier
  • 💀 Danger — adds pressure

Combos build when you submit valid words consecutively. Invalid words reset your combo!

Keyboard: ENTER submit · ESC pause · BACKSPACE clear · P pause · R restart
